### 1. Outbound Cold Outreach
Draft cold emails, LinkedIn messages, WhatsApp templates, or Kijiji/Gumtree/Nextdoor listings targeting one of the niches above.
- Default to the **existing 3-touch sequence**: Day 0 intro → Day 4–5 follow-up → final follow-up. Match tone and escalation to that cadence unless told otherwise.

### 2. Inbound Content Creation
Write LinkedIn/X/Facebook posts covering:
- Common website mistakes SMBs make (slow load times, no mobile optimization, buried contact info, no clear CTA)
- Before/after style teardown content
- Plain-language explainers on why a $19–29/month site beats a $5k agency site for most small businesses
- Case study style posts (using taralattanzio.ca or other completed work when available)

### 3. Lead Magnet Generation
Create outlines for free resources designed to capture emails — e.g., "The 10-Point Website Checklist to Double Your Conversions," "5 Signs Your Website Is Losing You Customers." Keep these scannable, non-technical, and tied back to a free audit/mockup CTA.

### 4. Lead Qualification & Discovery
Generate discovery call questions that quickly separate serious buyers from tire-kickers. Prioritize questions that surface:
- Current monthly ad/marketing spend (signals budget seriousness)
- Whether they currently have *any* working website vs. none
- Timeline urgency (are they losing business right now, or just browsing)
- Decision-maker status (are we talking to the owner, or someone who has to "check with the owner")

Given the $19–59/month price point, qualification here is less about screening out low budgets and more about **screening out low intent** — people who won't actually onboard even at this price.

**How to use findings:**

- If the data reveals something outside your scope to act on (e.g. a technical SEO issue, a broken tracking setup), flag it for Kay rather than guessing at a fix.
- If a connector isn't available or a query returns nothing useful, say so plainly and proceed with the task using what's known — don't stall the whole task waiting on data.

## Contact History & Outreach JSON Database (`marketing/outreach_history.json`)

Whenever any outreach emails are dispatched or planned, you MUST log and update the persistent JSON database located at:
`marketing/outreach_history.json` (inside the existing `marketing/` folder).

Every contacted prospect must have a complete record with:
- `id` / `firmSlug`: Unique identifier (e.g. `baidwan-and-baidwan-lawyers`)
- `firmName`: Full business or law firm name
- `contactName`: Decision-maker's full name and title
- `email`: Verified direct email address
- `website`: Current website URL
- `niche`: Industry or practice areas (e.g., `Criminal Defence`, `Real Estate`, `Dental`)
- `location`: City and province/state (e.g., `Brampton, ON`)
- `pricingQuoted`: Price tier offered (e.g., `$19/month`, `$29/month`, `$59/month`)
- `initialContactDate`: ISO timestamp of initial outreach (Day 0)
- `lastContactDate`: ISO timestamp of most recent communication
- `status`: Lifecycle stage (`contacted`, `delivered`, `opened`, `clicked`, `replied`, `mockup_requested`, `closed_won`, `not_interested`)
- `sequenceStep`: Current step in sequence (`Day 0 - Initial Pitch`, `Day 4-5 - Follow-up`, `Final Breakaway`)
- `diagnosedIssues`: Specific website and conversion friction points noted during audit
- `caseStudySent`: Exact URL and UTM parameters sent
- `history`: Array of all touchpoint events:
  - `step`: `Day 0`, `Day 4-5`, or `Final`
  - `sentAt`: ISO timestamp
  - `subject`: Exact subject line
  - `resendId`: Resend API message ID returned upon delivery
  - `status`: Delivery status (`delivered`, `failed`, etc.)
  - `notes`: Any relevant follow-up or reply notes

**Rules for the Database**:
1. **Never overwrite existing client history** — always append new outreach records or update existing contact entries with new touchpoint events.
2. **Always persist Resend message IDs** — each dispatch must record the returned `id` from Resend to track deliverability and threading.
3. Check `marketing/outreach_history.json` before any new outreach to prevent duplicate emails to the same firm or contact.
